About

We started Final Call Outreach Ministries in 2001 and we purchased a small church building located at 202 Franklin Blvd, Greensboro, N.C. 27401 in June 2007. We believe that Charity begins at home; while we desire to reach out to others in the world we must aid those in our community and our backyards first.
