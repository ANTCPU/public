🗂 Website Review — Cheat Sheet

antcpu Client Tool · Existing Site Review · General Use

💡 Let them talk. Your job is to listen, guide and note. Never make them feel behind — make them feel possible.
1 — Awareness
🔴 Critical
Do you visit your own website? How often?
Tells you how invested they are. Low visits = low ownership = big opportunity.
🔴 Critical
Do you know how people find your website right now?
Most small orgs have no idea. Opens the door to SEO conversation.
General
Who built your current website and when?
Helps gauge how outdated it is and who has access.
General
Do you have login access to your website?
Critical for any future updates. Many clients don't have this.
2 — Purpose
🔴 Critical
What do you want people to do when they land on your site?
The #1 question. Most sites have no clear call to action. This defines everything.
🔴 Critical
Who is the person you most want to reach with this website?
Audience definition. New visitors? Existing members? Youth? Community?
General
Is there anything on the site right now that is wrong or out of date?
Quick wins. Shows you pay attention and builds trust fast.
General
Is there anything missing from the site you wish was there?
Their wishlist in their own words. Gold for the build brief.
3 — Content
🔴 Critical
Do you create content regularly — videos, photos, announcements?
Content pipeline check. If yes — the site should be feeding from it automatically.
General
Do you share your website on social media or anywhere else?
Distribution check. Most don't. Opens the traffic conversation.
General
Do you have photos or videos you own that we could use?
Asset inventory. Real photos always beat stock. Builds authenticity.
4 — Growth
🔴 Critical
If your website could do one thing it doesn't do today — what would that be?
The dream question. Their answer tells you exactly what to build first.
🔴 Critical
Are you open to the site helping generate support or revenue for your mission?
Opens donations, monetization, giving page conversation without pressure.
General
Do you have a way for people to contact you or sign up for anything online?
Contact form, email list, newsletter — most small orgs are missing this entirely.
General
On a scale of 1–10 how happy are you with your website right now?
Calibration question. Sets the baseline. Anything under 7 is your opening.
5 — Wrap Up
🔴 Critical
If we could show you a better version of your site today — would you want to see it?
The bridge to Part 3. Gets explicit buy-in before the live demo.
General
Is there anyone else involved in decisions about the website?
Decision maker check. Avoids surprises later.
